Hobbies & Interests

These are some of the things I enjoy spending my time on.

Cybersecurity

I always had a huge interest in cybersecurity. I’ve always been impressed by how a single piece of code or a small bug can have such a significant impact.

Gaming

My favorite games are Watch Dogs, Left 4 Dead, and Minecraft. Those are the games I can play with mods for hours without getting bored.

Coding

I’m going very deep into coding. I started coding, and after a while, I got into cybersecurity. I realized that I needed to learn a lot more about programming because I was struggling to read code without understanding what it meant. But now, I genuinely love it, even though I don’t code as much as I’d like to admit.

Reading

Reading is so important. I struggle to read a lot, but I try my best to read philosophical books because I love philosophy and reasoning. I don’t really like short stories, movies, or series either. Books are okay in my opinion, though.

Languages

I speak three languages fluently, and I’m currently learning two others. I find it very useful when I travel, and I also enjoy being able to talk to people I don’t know. I’m still a beginner in the two languages I’m learning, but I really love learning new languages.

Technology

Cybersecurity is a fun passion of mine. I love everything about technology. I want to keep improving and become better at it than I am now.

Hardware

I know quite a bit about hardware since I own both a PC and a laptop. I understand how each component works and what its purpose is, and I’m always keeping up with the latest news about GPUs and CPUs.
