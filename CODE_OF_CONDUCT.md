# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Welcome to the 7DVD community.

This project is a personal website and knowledge space where ideas, projects, experiments, research, and learning experiences are shared. The goal of this community is to encourage learning, collaboration, constructive discussion, and the exchange of knowledge.

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone who chooses to participate.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- Treat others with respect, professionalism, and courtesy.
- Be welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Keep discussions constructive and focused on the subject being discussed.
- Provide useful and honest feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism without turning discussions into personal conflicts.
- Ask questions when something is unclear rather than making assumptions.
- Help newcomers and less experienced contributors where possible.
- Give appropriate credit to other people's work, ideas, and resources.
- Respect the privacy and personal information of other community members.
- Follow GitHub's Terms of Service and applicable policies.
- Contribute in good faith and with the intention of improving the project or community.

Technical disagreements are completely acceptable. Disagreement should remain focused on ideas, implementations, evidence, or technical decisions rather than becoming personal.

## Discussions

When participating in GitHub Discussions:

- Clearly explain the topic, question, or idea you want to discuss.
- Provide relevant context and information.
- Stay reasonably on topic.
- Avoid repeatedly posting the same question or discussion.
- Do not use discussions for advertising, unsolicited promotion, or spam.
- If you disagree with someone, explain your reasoning respectfully.
- Avoid personal attacks, insults, or hostile language.
- Do not intentionally derail conversations.
- When sharing external links, explain why the link is relevant.
- Use the appropriate discussion category when one is available.

A good discussion should help people learn, solve problems, exchange ideas, or improve the project.

## Issues

When opening an issue:

- Search existing issues first to avoid unnecessary duplicates.
- Clearly describe the problem, suggestion, or request.
- Include relevant information that may help reproduce or understand the issue.
- Use a descriptive title.
- Keep unrelated topics in separate issues.
- Do not use issues for personal disputes or general arguments.

Issues may be closed when they are resolved, duplicated, no longer relevant, or do not provide enough information to be actionable.

## Pull Requests and Contributions

Contributions are welcome when they are relevant to the project.

When submitting a Pull Request:

- Clearly explain what you changed and why.
- Keep changes focused and reasonably scoped.
- Follow the existing structure and conventions of the project where practical.
- Test your changes when applicable.
- Do not intentionally introduce malicious code, malware, backdoors, or other harmful functionality.
- Do not submit someone else's work without appropriate permission or attribution.
- Be open to review and reasonable suggestions from maintainers.
- Keep discussions about code constructive and professional.

Maintainers may request changes before a contribution is accepted.

## Cybersecurity and Security Research

This project may contain content related to cybersecurity, ethical hacking, security research, programming, and related technical subjects.

Security-related discussions must remain responsible and educational.

Do not use this project to:

- Promote malicious or illegal activity.
- Distribute malware, credentials, stolen data, or other harmful material.
- Publish private or sensitive information belonging to other people.
- Encourage unauthorized access to systems, accounts, networks, or services.
- Target real individuals, organizations, or systems without authorization.
- Use project discussions or contributions to facilitate abuse.

Security research, demonstrations, proof-of-concepts, and educational material should be presented with appropriate context and should not encourage or facilitate unauthorized harm.

If you discover a security issue affecting this project, please report it privately rather than publicly disclosing sensitive details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks or targeted insults.
- Trolling intended to disrupt or provoke others.
- Spam or repeated unwanted content.
- Malicious or deliberately disruptive contributions.
- Doxxing or sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Impersonating project maintainers or other community members.
- Deliberately spreading false or misleading information.
- Unsolicited advertising or excessive self-promotion.
- Publishing malicious code or content intended to harm others.
- Attempting to exploit, compromise, or abuse systems without authorization.
- Any other behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a professional community.

## Content Moderation

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, close, or restrict content that violates this Code of Conduct or otherwise negatively affects the project or its community.

Depending on the circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
3. Closing an issue, discussion, or pull request.
4. Temporary restriction from participating.
5. Permanent restriction or blocking from the project.

Enforcement decisions will be made based on the circumstances of each situation.

## Reporting a Violation

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainer through a private GitHub communication channel.

When reporting an incident, please provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

- A description of what happened.
- Links to the relevant issue, discussion, pull request, or other content.
- The GitHub usernames of the people involved.
- Any additional context that may help understand the situation.

Please do not publicly escalate a conflict or share private information while reporting an incident.

Reports will be reviewed as appropriate and handled with reasonable discretion.

## Good-Faith Participation

This Code of Conduct is intended to establish reasonable standards for a healthy and productive community.

Mistakes happen, and not every disagreement is a violation. Members are encouraged to communicate in good faith, assume positive intent where reasonable, and resolve misunderstandings constructively.

At the same time, repeated or deliberate violations may result in moderation or enforcement action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions associated with 7DVD, including but not limited to:

- GitHub Issues
- GitHub Discussions
- Pull Requests
- Repository contributions
- Project-related comments
- Project-related communication
- Other spaces where someone is representing the project or its community

## Disclaimer

The content of this project represents personal research, experiences, experiments, and opinions. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions and discussions should not be interpreted as professional advice, official security guidance, or authorization to perform activities against systems you do not own or have permission to test.

Always obtain appropriate authorization before conducting security testing or interacting with systems that you do not own.

## Final Note

The purpose of this community is to learn, build, experiment, share knowledge, and improve together.

Be respectful. Be constructive. Ask questions. Share what you learn. Help others when you can.

Thank you for helping make 7DVD a useful and welcoming place for everyone.
